What is paper trading?

Paper trading is a way to rehearse trade decisions in a simulated environment instead of placing orders with a live brokerage account. It can help you follow a written plan, practise a routine and notice habits in your decisions. It cannot reproduce every condition of real trading or guarantee future performance.

How to practise with MethodA

  1. Set a practice objective.

    Choose one setup or behaviour to observe before a session.

  2. Use the platform tools.

    Organise a session with available watchlists, charts and virtual capital.

  3. Review the simulation.

    Look at simulated profit and loss, capital use and the decisions behind each trade.

  4. Record one takeaway.

    Use your review to decide what to repeat or change in your next practice session.
