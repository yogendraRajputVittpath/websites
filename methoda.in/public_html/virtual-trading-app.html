What virtual trading can help you practise

A virtual trading app lets you rehearse market decisions without sending orders to a live brokerage account. You can define a practice goal, follow a planned approach, and review what happened in the simulation. Treat this as a learning exercise, not a forecast of live-market performance.

A simple practice loop

  1. Plan.

    Write down the setup and behaviour you want to practise before opening a session.

  2. Practise.

    Use virtual capital with the tools and instruments currently available in MethodA.

  3. Review.

    Look at simulated P&L, capital use and your decisions—not just whether a trade was profitable.

  4. Reflect.

    Choose one specific adjustment to test in your next session.

What MethodA offers

MethodA is designed for Indian stocks and F&O simulation and includes virtual-capital plans, watchlists, charts, market indices, top movers and trading review tools. The application is the source of truth for current instruments and market-data availability; we do not promise live market data on this page.

Virtual does not mean cost-free or risk-free

MethodA does not execute real-market orders. Virtual balances are not cash, and simulated profits cannot be withdrawn. Premium Account subscription fees may apply. Simulation also cannot reproduce all live-market conditions, so it cannot guarantee a particular outcome.
