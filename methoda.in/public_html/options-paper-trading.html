Practise the decision process, not just the outcome

Options and futures have characteristics that can make results sensitive to price movement, time, volatility, liquidity and contract details. A virtual session can be used to rehearse a checklist and record decisions, but simulation cannot reproduce every live-market condition.

A checklist for an F&O practice session

  • Write down the reason for the trade and what would invalidate the idea.
  • Set a planned exit and a maximum amount you are prepared to lose in the simulation.
  • Record the instrument and conditions shown in the app when you practise.
  • Review whether you followed your plan, including how you handled an unfavourable move.

Check which instruments are available

MethodA’s stated product scope includes F&O simulation, but this page does not promise a particular index, contract, option chain, order type or live-data feed. Confirm current supported instruments and data availability inside the application before relying on them for a practice exercise.

Understand the limits of virtual results

No real-market orders are placed through MethodA. Virtual balances are not withdrawable money, subscription fees may apply, and simulated profits do not establish that an approach will work in live markets. MethodA is not a brokerage or a provider of personalised investment advice.
