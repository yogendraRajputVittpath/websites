How a stock market simulator works

A simulator lets you rehearse market decisions using virtual funds instead of placing orders in a real brokerage account. It creates a space to practise a process, such as following a pre-defined entry and exit plan, while understanding that simulation is not the same as live execution.

Turn a practice session into a learning loop

  • Write down the market idea and the rule you plan to follow.
  • Use the available watchlists and charts to organise your simulated session.
  • Review simulated P&L, capital use and trade outcomes after the session.
  • Note whether you followed your plan, then select one adjustment to practise.

This kind of review can help you observe habits and test how consistently you follow a process. It does not show that a strategy will be profitable in real markets.

MethodA’s simulation scope

MethodA is designed for virtual trading in Indian stocks and F&O. Available instruments and market-data behaviour can vary; check the application for current details. MethodA does not place real-market orders, and virtual balances are not withdrawable funds. A Premium Account fee may apply.

Simulation has limits

Live trading may involve execution differences, financial pressure, transaction costs and other conditions that a simulator cannot fully reproduce. Treat simulated results as practice records, not a promise or prediction of future returns.
