About Vittyaan

Building trading technology platforms for a new generation of informed market participants

Vittyaan is a FinTech technology company focused on developing simulation-driven trading platforms. We build tools for practice, analysis, and systematic learning—not investment advice or recommendations.

Our Philosophy

Principles that guide our technology development

🎯

Process Over Prediction

We believe in systematic approaches and rule-based frameworks rather than predictions or market timing claims.

💻

Technology Over Tips

Our focus is building robust technology platforms, not providing trading tips or investment recommendations.

📚

Learning Before Capital

Practice with simulation and virtual capital to understand markets before deploying real money.

What We Build

✅ We Do

  • Build technology platforms for trading simulation
  • Develop paper trading and practice environments
  • Create analytics and performance tracking tools
  • Provide backtesting and historical simulation systems
  • Offer risk visualization and drawdown analysis
  • Enable user-controlled automation frameworks

❌ We Do NOT

  • Provide investment advisory services
  • Offer trading tips, calls, or recommendations
  • Manage funds or act as portfolio managers
  • Operate as brokers or intermediaries
  • Make profit claims or success guarantees
  • Provide automated buy/sell signals

Built by Technology Professionals

Vittyaan is developed by engineers and technology experts who understand both software systems and financial markets. Our team focuses on creating reliable, scalable platforms that prioritize user control and decision independence.

We maintain a strict separation between technology provision and advisory services, ensuring our platforms remain tools for learning and practice rather than sources of investment advice.

⚖️ Regulatory Clarity

Vittyaan is NOT registered with SEBI as an investment advisor, research analyst, broker, or portfolio manager.

We are a technology platform provider focused on simulation, education, and systematic trading infrastructure.

All our platforms are designed for learning, practice, and independent decision-making. Users are solely responsible for their trading choices and risk management.
